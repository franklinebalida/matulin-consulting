Common questions

Veterans' benefits questions

Can you help our school get approved for veterans' education benefits?

Yes. I help schools check eligibility, prepare the catalog and policies the approving agency expects, and assemble the application for the institution and its programs.

Our application was returned. Can you help?

Yes. We work through each item the agency raised, correct the catalog or supporting documents, and resubmit a complete package.

Can you serve as our School Certifying Official?

Yes. I provide contracted School Certifying Official support for schools that do not have a full-time certifying official on staff, handling certifications and keeping records ready for review.

Can you help us prepare for a compliance visit?

Yes. We review student files and certification records before the visit, fix discrepancies, and prepare staff for the reviewer's questions.
