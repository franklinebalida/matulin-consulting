How accreditation support works

Support that starts well before the site visit

The earlier we start, the more time your team has to fix what the review turns up. If your visit is already close, I can join at any stage.

  1. Readiness review

    We compare your policies, files, and outcomes data against the standards and rank the gaps by risk.

  2. Self-evaluation

    I help your team write or strengthen the report, with each statement tied to evidence a reviewer can find.

  3. Visit preparation

    We organize documents, sample student files, and prepare staff and leadership for team questions.

  4. After the visit

    I help you answer any findings with corrective action and proof, then set up a calendar for the next cycle.
