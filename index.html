How accreditation support works

Accreditation support that starts well before the site visit

The earlier we start, the more time your team has to fix what the review turns up. If your visit is already close, I can join at any stage.

  1. Readiness review

    We compare your policies, files, and outcomes data against the standards and rank the gaps by risk.

  2. Self-evaluation

    I help your team write or strengthen the report, with each statement tied to evidence a reviewer can find.

  3. Visit preparation

    We organize documents, sample student files, and prepare staff and leadership for team questions.

  4. After the visit

    I help you answer any findings with corrective action and proof, then set up a calendar for the next cycle.

FAQ

Accreditation consulting questions

What does an accreditation consultant do?

An accreditation consultant helps a school understand what its accreditor expects and prepare to meet it. That can include reviewing policies and student files, writing the self-evaluation report, organizing evidence, preparing staff for the site visit, and responding to any findings.

When should a school start preparing for reaccreditation?

Start well before your self-evaluation report is due, because fixing gaps in policies, files, and outcomes data takes time. The right start date depends on your accreditor's cycle. If your visit is already close, I can still join at any stage.

Can you help a school seeking accreditation for the first time?

Yes. I can review your readiness against the accreditor's standards, identify what needs to be in place first, and help your team build the policies, evidence, and application materials.

Do you work with my accreditor?

My direct experience is with national institutional accreditation of career and online schools, along with state and veterans' benefit approvals. Self-study, evidence, and visit preparation carry across agencies, so tell me which one you work with and I'll say plainly whether I'm the right fit.

Can you help if a regulator has sent us a notice?

Yes. I help schools respond to enrollment holds, deficiency notices, and agency inquiries by drafting the narrative response, assembling the evidence, and correcting the underlying documents. Contact me as soon as you receive the notice so we can work within its deadline.

Do you work with schools outside California?

Yes. I am based in San Diego, California, and work with schools across the United States remotely.

Can an accreditation consultant guarantee accreditation?

No. Accreditation and approval decisions rest with each agency, and no consultant can guarantee an outcome. My job is to help your institution present accurate, well-supported evidence and fix problems before a reviewer finds them.

Seeking accreditation, or facing a deadline?

Tell me your accreditor, your next deadline, and what concerns you most. I'll let you know whether and how I can help.
