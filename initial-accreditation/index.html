Common questions

Initial accreditation questions

Can a new school apply for accreditation right away?

Often not. Accreditors commonly expect a school to hold the required state approvals and to have a period of operating history before it applies. The requirements differ by agency, so the first step is to check them against where your school stands today.

How long does initial accreditation take?

It depends on the accreditor's process and how ready your school is when you begin. A readiness review gives you a realistic picture of the work ahead, which you can then build into a calendar with your team.

Which accreditor should our school choose?

That depends on your programs, how you deliver them, and your goals. Tell me what you have in mind, and I can help you compare requirements. I'll also say plainly if an agency falls outside my experience.

Can you guarantee that we will be accredited?

No. The decision rests with the accreditor, and no consultant can guarantee an outcome. My job is to help you present accurate, well-supported evidence and fix problems before a reviewer finds them.
