Common questions

Reaccreditation questions

When should we start preparing for reaccreditation?

Start well before your self-evaluation report is due, because fixing gaps in policies, files, and outcomes data takes time. The right start date depends on your accreditor's cycle.

Our site visit is coming up soon. Is it too late?

No. I can join at any stage. When time is short, we focus first on the gaps that carry the most risk and on getting your evidence and staff ready for the visit.

Can you help after we receive findings?

Yes. I help schools draft the response, assemble the evidence, and correct the underlying documents. Contact me as soon as you receive the findings so we can work within the deadline.

Can you guarantee that our accreditation will be renewed?

No. The decision rests with the accreditor, and no consultant can guarantee an outcome. My job is to help you present accurate, well-supported evidence and fix problems before a reviewer finds them.
