Help responding to a regulatory notice

Received an enrollment hold, a deficiency notice, or an agency inquiry? I help your team understand what the agency needs, fix the problems behind the notice, and submit a complete response before the deadline.

How I help

From the notice to a complete response

Notices come with deadlines. The sooner we start, the more time there is to fix the underlying problems instead of only explaining them.

Reading the notice

We pin down exactly what the agency is asking for, what it will accept as proof, and when each piece is due.

Finding the root cause

We trace each concern back to the policy, record, or practice behind it, so the fix holds up after the response is filed.

Correcting the documents

I revise the catalog, enrollment agreement, policies, and disclosures involved so they match the rules and your actual practice.

Writing the response

I draft a clear narrative response with your team, with each statement tied to evidence the agency can check.

Assembling the evidence

We gather and organize the exhibits, records, and corrected documents into a complete, easy-to-review package.

Preventing a repeat

We set up a compliance calendar and simple checks so the same issue does not come back at your next review.

The process

How we work through a notice

Every notice is different, but the work follows the same path from the first read to the final follow-up.

  1. Triage

    We confirm the deadline, the agency's concerns, and what proof each one needs.

  2. Fix

    We correct the documents and practices behind each concern.

  3. Respond

    I draft the response with your team and assemble the evidence package.

  4. Follow through

    We answer any follow-up questions and put checks in place for the future.

Common questions

Regulatory notice questions

We just received a notice. What should we do first?

Note the response deadline, read exactly what the agency is asking for, and avoid sending a quick reply that has not been checked against your documents. Then get help early, so there is time to fix the underlying problems rather than only describe them.

Can you help with an enrollment hold?

Yes. I help schools understand what the agency needs to see, correct the catalog, policies, and records involved, and submit a complete response so the school can work toward regaining its authority to enroll.

Do you deal with the agency for us?

Your school remains the party responsible to the agency and signs its own submissions. I prepare the response and evidence with your team and can help you plan calls and meetings with agency staff.

Can you guarantee the notice will be resolved?

No. The decision rests with the agency, and no consultant can guarantee an outcome. My job is to help you submit an accurate, well-supported response and fix the problems behind the notice.
